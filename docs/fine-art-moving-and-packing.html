How We Pack It

Fine Art Packing, Shipping, and Transport

Fine art packing and shipping requires corrugated fiberboard boxes and meticulous attention to detail to ensure artwork is protected from damage during transit. The packing process uses high-quality, archival materials designed to safeguard delicate surfaces and structural integrity. For paintings, this might include acid-free tissue paper, corner protectors, and custom-built crates lined with foam to prevent movement and absorb shock. Sculptures and three-dimensional pieces often require custom crating solutions, with inner supports and padding tailored to the artwork's shape and weight. Climate-sensitive pieces may also be packed with desiccants to control humidity and prevent deterioration, with packaging designed to withstand the rigors of transportation by air, sea, or ground.

Shipping fine art involves coordination with professional art couriers trained to handle valuable and fragile pieces. Logistics professionals use climate-controlled vehicles and secure loading techniques that minimize vibrations and potential impact. Comprehensive documentation, including condition reports and insurance, is prepared to track and protect the art throughout its journey, with GPS tracking and security measures like sealed crates and discreet labeling reducing the risk of loss or theft. This highly detailed approach ensures fine art reaches its destination safely and in the same condition it was packed in.
