What Is Freight Shipping?

Freight shipping moves larger, heavier loads by truck, rail, air, or ocean, with trucking handling most domestic moves thanks to its door-to-door flexibility. Unlike parcel services for packages under roughly 150 lbs, freight is built for palletized, crated, or consolidated shipments, which means better economics and more specialized handling.

How Freight Shipping Works

Reliable pickup and delivery are the backbone of freight shipping. We offer:

  • Door-to-door service: direct from origin to destination, ideal for time-sensitive or high-value goods.
  • White-glove options: extra care for fragile, valuable, or oversized items like art, antiques, electronics, or casino equipment.
  • Scheduled or expedited pickups: same-day or next-day for urgent needs.
  • Liftgate and inside delivery: for locations without loading docks.

These services reduce hassle, minimize damage risk, and keep deliveries on time.

Storage Built Into Your Freight Plan

Short- and long-term storage bridges gaps in your supply chain:

  • Secure, climate-controlled warehouse space for sensitive items like art, wine, and electronics.
  • Pallet racking and organized space for efficient inventory management.
  • Temporary holding during transit delays, seasonal peaks, or before final delivery.

Goods can be picked up, stored briefly, then shipped out, so you don't need dedicated warehouse space of your own.

Small Freight Moves: LTL and Flexible Options

Not every shipment needs a full truck. Small freight moves, from a single pallet to several, use Less Than Truckload (LTL) shipping, where multiple shippers share truck space:

  • Pay only for the space and weight you use.
  • Ship items too big for parcel carriers but too small for a dedicated truck.
  • Scale up to Full Truckload (FTL) as your needs grow.
