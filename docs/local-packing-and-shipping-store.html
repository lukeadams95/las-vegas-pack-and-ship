International Shipping

Saving time and money from Las Vegas

Shipping overseas can be daunting, but handling everything in-house simplifies it — consolidating these steps shaves days off transit time and reduces cost.

01

Small parcels, handled end to end

Shipping that signed guitar pick to a fan in Australia means a custom box, customs paperwork completed — like the CN22 declaration — and the best carrier selected for cost efficiency.

02

Large, compliant crated freight

For the slot machine headed to Japan, crates are built compliant with ISPM-15 wood treatment standards, Electronic Export Information (EEI) is filed if the value exceeds $2,500, and rates are negotiated directly with carriers — no third-party broker delays or markups.

03

Fine art across borders

Fine art going abroad, like the sculpture to Paris, gets coordination with customs brokers, Certificates of Origin secured if needed, and packaging that meets destination regulations. Handling this internally can cut costs by 20–30% and beat chain-store timelines by days.
