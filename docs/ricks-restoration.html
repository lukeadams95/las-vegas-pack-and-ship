Behind the Scenes

Pallets and Crates in the Workflow

At Rick's Restorations, the renowned Las Vegas shop featured on American Restoration, pallets and wood crates play a practical role in the day-to-day workflow of handling large, heavy, and often fragile vintage items. The team frequently receives oversized antiques — gas pumps, vending machines, jukeboxes, or barber chairs — delivered on sturdy wooden pallets that provide a stable base for transport and initial inspection upon arrival.

These pallets let the crew maneuver heavy pieces safely around the workshop using forklifts or dollies, preventing damage to restored surfaces or delicate components during the early disassembly stages. Once projects are complete, the same pallets (or custom-built wood crates) become essential for secure packaging, ensuring painstakingly revived pieces survive shipping to excited clients without scratches, dents, or structural harm.

Resourceful by Nature

A Touch of Ingenuity

Beyond logistics, the rustic character of reclaimed pallets and crates occasionally inspires creative touches in restorations or shop operations. Rick Dale and his team, known for their resourceful approach to salvaging materials, might repurpose sections of weathered pallet wood for temporary jigs, supports during welding, or even rustic display elements that complement the vintage aesthetic of many projects.

Custom wood crates, often hand-crafted or sourced through specialized partners for international shipments, add an extra layer of protection for high-value items heading overseas. This blend of practicality and ingenuity keeps the restoration process efficient, cost-effective, and true to the shop's hands-on, no-nonsense style that made the business a fan favorite.
