What Does an Art Courier Service Provide

An art courier service is a specialized transportation service dedicated to the safe and secure handling, transport, and delivery of valuable artwork. These services cater to museums, galleries, artists, collectors, and auction houses, ensuring that delicate and often priceless pieces of art are moved with the highest standards of care and security.

01

Specialized Handling

Trained professionals understand the unique requirements of handling artwork, taking extra precautions to prevent damage in transit. Handling techniques minimize exposure to environmental risks like temperature changes, humidity, vibrations, and physical impact.

02

Packing and Crating

Proper packing is critical to protecting artwork from damage. We provide custom packing and crating using materials that cushion and secure the art — delicate paintings wrapped in acid-free paper and encased in climate-controlled crates, sculptures bolted securely into custom-built containers.

03

Climate Control & Environmental Protection

Many artworks are sensitive to temperature and humidity. Climate-controlled transport maintains a stable environment, especially important for oil paintings, antique manuscripts, or works on paper.

04

Security and Risk Management

Strict security measures protect artwork from theft or tampering, including GPS tracking of vehicles, secure loading and unloading, and discreet transport methods, with training in risk management and crisis response.

05

Documentation and Condition Reporting

Thorough documentation, including condition reports before and after transportation, verifies that no damage occurred in transit — essential for insurance purposes and as a record of the artwork's condition.

06

Customs and Legal Assistance

For international shipments, we handle the complex customs and legal requirements: preparing and filing paperwork, obtaining permits, and ensuring compliance with export and import regulations.

07

Personalized and White-Glove Service

Every detail of the transport is carefully managed, with couriers able to accompany the artwork from pickup to delivery for exceptional, hands-on care.

08

Coordination With Art Institutions

We work closely with museums, galleries, auction houses, and private collectors to coordinate every aspect of transport — scheduling, installation and deinstallation, and collaboration with curators or conservators.

09

Insurance Coverage

Given the high value of the artwork being transported, insurance coverage is a critical component, tailored to the value and risk of the specific piece being moved.
